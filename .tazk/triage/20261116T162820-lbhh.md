Code review: num-bigint is unnecessary for base36 encoding of at most 5 bytes

Where: src/hash.rs encode_base36; Cargo.toml num-bigint and num-traits.

Problem: encode_base36 uses BigUint for inputs of at most 5 bytes (40 bits), which fit in a u64. Lengths 7 and 8 both use 5 bytes, so 8-character IDs have about 7.7 characters of entropy (leading digit is nearly always 0).

Suggested fix: convert with u64::from_be_bytes on a padded buffer and plain % 36 arithmetic; drop num-bigint and num-traits; use 6 bytes for length 8 if the extra entropy is wanted.
