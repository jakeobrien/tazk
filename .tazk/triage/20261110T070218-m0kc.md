Make `role` optional for `epic` and `spec` issues

`tz create` and `tz triage promote` currently require `-r/--role` for every issue type. Under the solo-directed-agents workflow (docs/workflows/solo-directed-agents.md), `role` means "who picks this up as work". Epics and specs never come back from `tz ready` (unless asked for with `--type`), so they are never picked up. An epic's owner is really the owners of its children, and a spec belongs to the planning session that wrote it. Being forced to choose a role for them adds noise, and it makes `tz list -r human` / `-r agent` less useful.

Proposed rule: `role` is required exactly for the types `tz ready` can return (`task`, `bug`, `feature`, `chore`, `gate`) and optional for `epic` and `spec`. The same rule applies on `create`, `triage promote`, `reopen` (which currently refuses an issue with no role), `update --issue-type` (changing an epic to a task with no role must require one), and `update --clear-role`.

Open question: should `role` be *forbidden* on epic/spec, or just optional? Optional is the gentler change.
